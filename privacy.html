Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to NumGuess ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website and application. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, use, and protection.

2. Information We Collect

NumGuess is a client-side game application that operates entirely in your web browser. We collect minimal personal information:

  • Local Storage Data: Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device only
  • Log Data: Basic server logs may include IP addresses and page access times
  • No Personal Information: We do not collect your name, email, or other personal identifiers
  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not use cookies or tracking technologies

3. How We Use Your Information

Since NumGuess operates primarily on your device, any data collected is used solely for:

  • Maintaining your game progress and preferences
  • Improving server performance and security
  • Analyzing usage patterns to enhance user experience

4. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your information:

  • All game data is stored locally on your device
  • HTTPS encryption for secure data transmission
  • No sensitive data is transmitted to external servers

5. Third-Party Services

NumGuess uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: For typography (Poppins font)
  • Font Awesome: For icons and symbols
  • CDN Services: For delivering static assets

These services may collect minimal usage data according to their own privacy policies. Please review their policies for more information.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear your browser's local storage to reset all game data
  • Disable JavaScript to prevent any data collection
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

7. Children's Privacy

NumGuess is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date below. Your continued use of NumGuess constitutes your acceptance of any changes.
